Privacy Policy for Randomly RemindMe

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how Randomly RemindMe, an Android app by James Morris Studios, handles your information.

Summary

James Morris Studios does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information from Randomly RemindMe. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your reminders, their messages, images and logs, and your settings are stored on your device. Backups and exports are saved only where you choose to save them.

This information is removed when you uninstall the app. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new device. That backup is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings. James Morris Studios never receives this data.

Location

Location-based reminders use your device's location to check whether you are in a reminder's area. This happens on your device and your location is not sent anywhere. The app only uses location if you set up a location-based reminder and grant the location permission.

Maps

When you pick a location for a reminder, the map is loaded from OpenStreetMap's tile servers. Those servers receive the normal information of a web request, such as your IP address and which map area is shown, and handle it under the OpenStreetMap Foundation Privacy Policy.

RSS feeds and reminder packs

If a reminder uses an RSS feed for its messages, the app downloads that feed from the address you entered. If you open a link to a reminder pack, the app downloads that file. No information about you is sent with these requests beyond what any web request includes.

Automation apps

If you set a reminder to send an Android intent to an automation app, the app sends only the intent you configured, to the app you chose.

Permissions

  • Notifications, alarms and vibration: to deliver reminders at their scheduled times
  • Run at startup: to reschedule reminders after the device restarts
  • Location: only for location-based reminders
  • Internet and network state: for maps, RSS feeds and reminder packs
  • Storage: for images, backups and exports you choose

Children

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@jamesmorrisstudios.us.
