Privacy Policy for BandIt Five

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This policy explains how BandIt Five, an Android app by James Morris Studios, handles your information.

Summary

James Morris Studios does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information from BandIt Five. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your settings are stored on your device.

This information stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Online play

Online play, saved games, achievements and leaderboards used Google Play Games services that Google has since retired, so they are currently unavailable. The app does not connect to Google Play Games or any other online service.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: declared for online play, which is currently unavailable

Children

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@jamesmorrisstudios.us.
