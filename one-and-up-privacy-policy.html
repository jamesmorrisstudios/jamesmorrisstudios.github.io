Privacy Policy for One & Up

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how One & Up, an Android app by James Morris Studios, handles your information.

Summary

James Morris Studios does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information from One & Up. The app has no ads, analytics or tracking.

Information stored on your device

Your game in progress, high scores, statistics and settings are stored on your device.

This information is removed when you uninstall the app. If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's data in your device backup in your Google account and restore it when you reinstall the app or set up a new device. That backup is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy, and you can turn it off in your device's backup settings. James Morris Studios never receives this data.

Google Play Games

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the app unlocks your achievements and submits your scores to its leaderboards. That information is sent to and stored by Google as part of your Play Games profile, and is handled under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games settings.

Sharing

The share button opens Android's share sheet. Whatever you choose to share is sent with the app you pick, under that app's own policy.

Permissions

The app does not request any device permissions.

Children

Because the app does not collect personal information, it does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@jamesmorrisstudios.us.
